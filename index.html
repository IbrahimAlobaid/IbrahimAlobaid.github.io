I design AI systems that hold up in the real world — from clinical workflows that read cardiac device data to voice agents that speak three languages — and I spend the rest of my time building the people who build them.

I'm an AI engineer with more than four years of hands-on experience across healthcare AI, real-time voice, compliance automation and computer vision. Today I lead the 0-to-1 build of an AI-powered remote monitoring platform for cardiac implantable devices at Cardiac Rhythm in the United States.

Outside of work I founded the Syrian AI Community, which launched Syria's first and second AI Camps, and co-founded the Aleppo Dev Community. I believe AI is learned by building something real — so that is how I teach it.

  1. i. Healthcare AI Clinical workflows, automated reporting and human-in-the-loop decision support.
  2. ii. Real-time Voice AI Low-latency multilingual agents with streaming STT/TTS and natural turn-taking.
  3. iii. Compliance & Data Systems AML screening, entity resolution and fault-tolerant ETL at scale.
  4. iv. LLMs, Agents & Vision RAG, multi-agent systems, document intelligence and visual perception.
